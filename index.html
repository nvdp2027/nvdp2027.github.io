Ten Years & Forever

Our story began long before we started dating. Although we first knew of each other in middle school, it wasn't until high school that we became close friends. That friendship eventually grew into something much deeper, and on October 3, 2016, we officially began our journey together.

Over the next decade, we grew up side by side, sharing milestones, navigating new adventures, and building a foundation of laughter, support, and friendship that defines our relationship.

On December 5, 2025, we took the next step in our story and got officially engaged. Now, we are so excited to celebrate this next chapter and start our forever with the people who mean the most to us.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code?

The dress code for our wedding is Black-Tie Required. We ask that men wear a tuxedo and women wear a formal floor-length gown.

Are children invited?

While we love your little ones, our wedding will be an adults-only event. We kindly request that you make childcare arrangements for the evening so you can celebrate with us!

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to venue capacity limits, we can only accommodate those guests formally listed on your wedding invitation. If you received a plus one, it will be specified on your invitation.

Is there parking available at Serra Plaza?

Yes, there is plentiful parking available onsite at Serra Plaza. However, we recommend using rideshare services (Uber/Lyft) if you plan on celebrating with us and drinking.
